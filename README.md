# Data Engineer

### Introduction
Welcome to my Data Engineering Portfolio! I'm a Data Engineer at Spot Inc., where I help build and maintain scalable data pipelines that support logistics and operational insights. I work primarily in Databricks using Spark, SQL, and Python, and I’m passionate about designing efficient systems that make data accessible and actionable.

Outside of work, I’m pursuing a Master’s in Data Science at the University of St. Thomas in Minnesota. My academic journey complements my hands on experience and fuels my drive to deepen my understanding of distributed systems, real time processing, and cloud architectures.

This portfolio is a collection of projects and experiences that reflect both my technical abilities and my curiosity to solve real world problems with data. Whether it’s automating a streaming pipeline or optimizing a Spark job, I approach each challenge with a mindset geared toward impact, reliability, and learning.

Thank you for stopping by I hope this gives you a clear picture of what I’ve done and where I’m heading.
